FAQ

Good questions.

Do you really come to me?

Yes. We’re a mobile mechanic — we come directly to your location to repair your vehicle: home, work, a parking lot or a jobsite.

What are your hours?

Our Google listing shows us as open 24 hours. Call or text any time — day, night or weekend.

Can I buy my own parts?

Yes. We primarily provide and warranty the labor portion of installing client-provided auto parts, and we can help you pick the right part for your car.

What areas do you cover?

We serve Jacksonville, Florida and the surrounding Duval area. Call with your location and we’ll confirm.

How do I pay?

We accept credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments (tap-to-pay).

What kinds of jobs do you do?

Customers most often call us for diagnostics, starter replacement, serpentine belts and oil changes, plus breakdowns at home, work or in a parking lot. Call to describe your job.

How much will it cost?

Every job is different, so we quote per job. Reviewers regularly describe our pricing as fair and reasonable.
